# Rate of penetration: introductory notes

Rate of penetration (ROP) describes how quickly a bit drills through a formation, commonly expressed in feet per hour or metres per hour. ROP is influenced by formation strength and abrasiveness, bit design and wear, weight on bit (WOB), rotary speed, hydraulics and hole cleaning, and drilling practices. These factors interact; changing one operating parameter may not improve ROP if another constraint dominates.

ROP is a performance measure, not a stand-alone measure of drilling safety or well integrity. Any operational decision must follow the approved well program and be made by qualified personnel using current well data.
