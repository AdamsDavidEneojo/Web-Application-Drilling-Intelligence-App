# Drilling signals: beginner glossary

Weight on bit (WOB) is the axial force applied to the bit. Rotary speed is the rate at which the drill string and bit rotate, often reported in revolutions per minute. Flow rate describes drilling fluid circulation. Drilling fluid helps transport cuttings out of the hole and supports other well functions. Torque is the rotational force on the drill string; standpipe pressure is a surface pressure measurement associated with circulating fluid.

These measurements provide context for understanding drilling performance, but their meaning depends on equipment, formation, depth, and the well plan. Example values in tutorials should not be treated as operating limits.
